EARTH — DAY SURFACE
NASA Visible Earth, Blue Marble Next Generation: Topography and Bathymetry
(December 2004, 5400 × 2700).
https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/73909
Usage: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
Resized to 3072 and 1536 px and converted to WebP
(public/assets/orbit/earth-day-*.webp).

EARTH — CITY LIGHTS
NASA Earth Observatory, Black Marble 2016 (0.1° and 3 km global composites).
https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/features/NightLights
Background land luminance removed so only emitted light remains. The global
map is packed into the red channel of public/assets/orbit/earth-data-*.webp; a
South Asia crop (40°E–120°E, 15°S–45°N) from the 3 km composite is
public/assets/orbit/lights-southasia-2k.webp.

EARTH — CLOUDS
NASA Visible Earth, Blue Marble: Clouds (cloud_combined_2048).
https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57747
Packed into the green channel of public/assets/orbit/earth-data-*.webp. The blue
channel is an ocean mask derived from the day surface.

ORIGINAL WORK
Atmosphere scattering, stars, lens response, film grain, guitar neck,
Karplus–Strong synthesis and every other visual in this site are original,
generated in code (src/scenes). Poster images in public/images/orbit are
renders of that scene. Project images are screenshots of the actual CJ OS and
CJ Universe builds. No NASA logos are included.
